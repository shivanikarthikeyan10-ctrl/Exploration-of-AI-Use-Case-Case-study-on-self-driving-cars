# Exploration-of-AI-Use-Case-Case-study-on-self-driving-cars
# AIM
To study the application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in self-driving cars and understand how AI technologies help vehicles perceive their surroundings, make decisions, and navigate safely without continuous human control.
# THEORY

Self-driving cars are autonomous vehicles that use Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), sensors, and computer vision to navigate roads and operate with limited or no human intervention. These vehicles use cameras, radar, LiDAR, GPS, and other sensors to detect surrounding objects, recognize traffic signs, identify pedestrians, and monitor road conditions.

AI algorithms analyze the collected data to understand the driving environment and make decisions such as steering, accelerating, braking, and changing lanes. Self-driving technology aims to improve road safety, reduce human errors, and provide efficient transportation.

# WORKING PRINCIPLE

The basic working process of a self-driving car is:

1. Sensors → Data Collection → Object Detection → Environment Understanding → Path Planning → Decision Making → Vehicle Control

2. Sensors such as cameras, radar, and LiDAR collect information about the vehicle's surroundings.

3. Computer vision and AI algorithms process the collected data.

4. The system identifies roads, pedestrians, vehicles, traffic signs, and obstacles.

5. The vehicle estimates its position and understands the surrounding environment.

6. Path-planning algorithms determine a suitable route and driving trajectory.

7. The AI system decides when to accelerate, brake, steer, or change lanes.

8. The vehicle control system executes these decisions through the steering, braking, and acceleration mechanisms.

9. The sensors continuously monitor the surroundings, allowing the system to update its decisions as road conditions change.

Example: When a self-driving car detects a pedestrian crossing the road, its AI system identifies the pedestrian, predicts the possible movement, and determines whether braking is necessary. The vehicle then applies the brakes to avoid a collision when required.

# RESULT
The study of AI-based self-driving cars explains how Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, computer vision, and sensor technologies work together to enable autonomous navigation. These systems can detect obstacles, interpret road conditions, plan driving paths, and control vehicle movements. Self-driving technology has the potential to improve transportation efficiency and reduce accidents caused by human error. However, challenges such as unpredictable road conditions, adverse weather, sensor limitations, and safety concerns remain important considerations.
